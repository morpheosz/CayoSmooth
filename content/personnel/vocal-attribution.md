The Expedition did not carry a singer. It carried a producer who sang,
four musicians who sometimes sang, and a standing arrangement for
engaging vocalists at ports of call.

The arrangement is documented in the ledger, which records payments of a
category the bookkeeper entered as "settled ashore." The category is used
fourteen times. In every instance the sum is small, the date is given,
the port is given, and the payee is not. The Archive's understanding,
which is inference and is labelled as such, is that vocal engagements
were how the Expedition discharged obligations it had incurred in
port — bar accounts, dockage, and in two cases a debt the ledger
describes only as "the other matter."

Against fourteen payments, the tape boxes carry seven port credits. The
Archive has not reconciled these figures and does not expect to.

## The distribution as catalogued

- **Vandermeer** — thirty-eight tracks.
- **Petrosky** — four, plus backing vocals throughout.
- **Ashe** — one, at his own request, per a note on the box.
- **Engaged at ports** — seven.
- **No credit recorded** — eleven.

## The seven

Six of the seven port credits give a name. Five of those names appear in
no other document held by the Archive, in any archive contacted, or in
the union rolls for the relevant years. They were paid in cash in a
foreign port by a bookkeeper who did not write down who they were.

The exceptions are two.

**R. Solís**, credited at Mazatlán, appears again eleven tracks later
under the same hand, which suggests either a second engagement or a
copying error, and the Archive has no way to distinguish between them.

**Esperanza**, credited at an unstated port, presents a different
difficulty. The liner notes to "Marina, Don't" identify its subject as
"Marina Esperanza," a facility for which the port authority has no record
at any point between 1965 and the present. The Archive notes the
recurrence of the name and declines to draw a conclusion from it. There
are a great many things named Esperanza.

The seventh credit is not a name. The box reads:

> GUEST (PAPAGAYO)

## Position

Attribution across the trove is incomplete. The Archive considers the
project ongoing and has considered it ongoing since 1994. It has been
suggested that the eleven uncredited tracks could be assigned by ear. The
Archive has declined, on the grounds that a credit assigned by ear is not
a credit, it is an opinion in a field where the Archive has already been
wrong twice.
