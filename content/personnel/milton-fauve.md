The Expedition's physician of record, aboard in a dental capacity. His
licence had lapsed in 1976 and was not renewed. The charter manifest
lists him as "Dr. Fauve, dentist," which is two-thirds accurate and was
never challenged by the port authority, the insurer, or anyone aboard.

There is no record of any dental procedure being performed during the
fourteen months. There is also no record of one being required. The
Archive presents both facts without preference.

Fauve's principal documented contribution is the ninety-second inventory
passage in "Anchorage," in which a warm baritone lists boat parts. The
session log credits him. The tape box credits Vandermeer. Fauve, when
approached in 1997, said he had "helped out on the long one" and declined
to be more specific, on the grounds that it had been a long time and he
had not been paid.

He was not paid. The Archive has confirmed this against the Expedition's
ledger, which records payments to four musicians and no one else.

Fauve returned with the others. He has never been the subject of a
claim, a dispute, or a licensing enquiry, and is the only member of the
Expedition of whom this is true.
