Referenced once, in the final legible entry of Vandermeer's journal:

> Sending Terry back with the tapes and the good blender.

This is the whole of the documentary record. Terry appears in no
manifest, no ledger, no session log, and no tape box. Terry is credited
on none of the sixty-one tracks. Terry is not among the four studio
musicians, all four of whom were paid and none of whom was named Terry.

The Archive's position on Terry is as follows.

Terry existed. The tapes came back. The blender came back, eventually,
by a route no one has reconstructed. Something conveyed them, and
Vandermeer, writing at the time, called it Terry.

Terry has not been identified. The Archive has pursued eleven candidate
identifications since 1991. Three were withdrawn by the persons proposing
them. Six were eliminated on documentary grounds. Two were eliminated on
the grounds that the candidate, when contacted, was not Terry.

The Archive is aware that this file is unsatisfying. It has been asked
several times to speculate and has consistently declined. Speculation
would close the file, and the file is not closed.
