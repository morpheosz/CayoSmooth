Session bassist. Credited on all sixty-one tracks. The nickname predates
the Expedition and is generally understood to describe his playing,
though no contemporaneous source states this and Petrosky, asked
directly in 1991, said it did not.

Petrosky is the Archive's origin point. He mentioned the tapes once, at a
boat show in Fort Lauderdale, to a member of what would become the
recovery team, allegedly while eating shrimp with both hands. The detail
about the shrimp appears in every retelling and in no signed statement.
The Archive reproduces it because it has been reproduced.

He was cooperative in the years that followed and imprecise. He confirmed
the crate. He confirmed Panama. He could not confirm the year, and gave
three different ones across four conversations, none of which was 1979.

On the subject of Terry he was consistent and unhelpful. He agreed there
was a Terry. He did not agree to characterise Terry further. Asked in
1994 whether Terry was among the four studio musicians, he said that
depended on how you counted, and then asked whether the Archive had
eaten.

Retired. The bass has not been located.
