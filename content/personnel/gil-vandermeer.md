title: "Gil Vandermeer"

role: "A&R; charter principal; vocals"

---

A&R man. Chartered the fifty-two-foot ketch in 1979, assembled the
personnel, secured the provisions, and set the Expedition's course by
telegram. The telegram survives. It gives the destination as "the smooth
latitudes" and does not give a bearing.

Vandermeer's professional record before 1979 is thin and unremarkable.
He signed acts. Some of them recorded. Nothing in the file anticipates
what followed, which the Archive has come to regard as the most
interesting thing about the file.

He is the credited vocalist on all sixty-one tracks. He is also, per the
original tape box, the credited speaker on the inventory passage in
"Anchorage," a credit the session log assigns to another party. The
Archive holds both documents and has declined to arbitrate between them.

He did not return. This is stated plainly in the Archive's records and
is not, at present, qualified. The journal's final entry has Vandermeer
sending Terry back with the tapes and the blender, which means Vandermeer
was somewhere at the time of writing and had decided not to be sent back
himself.

The Archive receives, on average, two letters a year proposing that
Vandermeer is alive. It answers all of them. It has never been persuaded
by one.
