The charter documentation records that the ketch departed Fort Lauderdale
carrying Vandermeer, an unlicensed dentist, and four studio musicians.
The figure of four is stated in the charter, in the insurer's schedule,
and in the Expedition's ledger, which records four payments.

The four are:

- **Lonnie Ashe** — Fender Rhodes. Credited on all sixty-one tracks.
- **Duane "The Cushion" Petrosky** — bass, backing vocals. Credited on
  all sixty-one tracks.
- **Ray Kettering** — drums. Credited on all sixty-one tracks.
- **Sonny Delgado** — saxophone. Credited on nine.

The Archive is aware that its own catalogue entry for "Chardonnay
Diplomacy" describes the roster as listing three names plus Delgado's, a
formulation that implies Delgado was an addition to a complete set rather
than a member of it. That entry has not been amended. The catalogue is a
primary document of the Archive's own process and the Archive does not
revise its process retroactively.

Ashe and Kettering returned. Neither has given an interview. Kettering
declined in writing in 1993, in a letter of one sentence, which the
Archive holds and considers the most substantive statement either has
made.

Delgado returned. The saxophone solo did not.

Terry is not on this roster. See `terry.md`.
