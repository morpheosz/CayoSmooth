The Archive's first release from the trove. The lyric addresses a marina
directly, by name, for the full six minutes, and asks it, repeatedly, not
to. The marina in question is identified in the liner notes as "Marina
Esperanza." Port authority records for the region list no marina under
that name at any point between 1965 and the present. The song is
unaffected by this and continues to address it.

Structurally the piece is a single extended verse with no bridge.
Vandermeer's vocal is doubled an octave down for the final two minutes, a
choice the restoration team attributes to either a deliberate arrangement
decision or a tape machine running at the wrong speed during a splice.
Both explanations are currently filed as equally likely.

The Rhodes part does not resolve. It is generally agreed this is the
point.
