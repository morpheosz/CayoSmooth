Nine verses, no chorus, extensively apologetic. The transcribed lyric
sheet held by the Archive contains eight. The ninth is referenced in
Vandermeer's session notes as "the one about the anchor windlass, cut for
time" and its absence from the tape is treated by the Archive as
confirmation that it was, in fact, recorded.

The apology is never specified. Petrosky's backing vocal, audible under
the final two verses, repeats a single word that the restoration team has
transcribed three different ways and adopted none of.

Frequently requested at Archive social functions, where it is understood
to apply to whatever the requester needs it to.
