Seventy-three-key electric piano, loaded aboard the ketch at Fort
Lauderdale in 1979 and audible on all sixty-one recovered tracks.

The instrument did not come back. Neither did the ketch. The Archive
notes that these are two separate facts and asks that they not be
combined into an assumption.

The Rhodes is nonetheless catalogued, and catalogued as an artifact
rather than as a loss, on the following grounds: the instrument is
present in the recordings, continuously, for fourteen months. The Archive
holds fourteen months of it. A holding is a holding.

Tuning drifts flat across the trove by approximately a quarter tone,
consistent with humidity and consistent with the passage of time. It does
not drift evenly. On four tracks it is sharp. Those four tracks are not
sequential and share no other property the Archive has been able to
identify.

Persons claiming to have seen the instrument after 1980 have contacted
the Archive on six occasions. Two of those accounts place it in Panama.
One places it in a hotel bar in Sarasota. The Archive has not travelled
to Sarasota.
