Bound notebook, marbled cover, approximately 180 leaves, of which 44 are
legible in full and 19 in part. The remainder are water-damaged. The
damage is uniform across the volume, which is inconsistent with a single
immersion event and has never been satisfactorily explained.

The journal is the Expedition's only first-person account and it is
unhelpful. Entries are undated. Roughly a third concern weather. A
further third concern provisioning, in a level of detail the Archive has
elected not to reproduce. The remainder are about smoothness and are
difficult to follow.

The final legible entry reads, in full:

> Reached the smoothness. It goes further. Sending Terry back with the
> tapes and the good blender.

This is the sole documentary reference to Terry. It is also the last
thing Vandermeer is known to have written. Scholarship on the entry has
concentrated almost entirely on the word "further," which the Archive
considers a misallocation of attention.

Digitisation was completed in 2004. The scans are held offsite. Requests
to consult the original are considered on a case-by-case basis and have
so far been considered.
