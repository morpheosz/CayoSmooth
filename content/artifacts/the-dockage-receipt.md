Transient slip receipt, one leaf, 108 × 178 mm, printed in two colours on
a card stock consistent with commercial job printing of the period.
Perforated along the upper edge, removed from a duplicate book. Folded
twice, horizontally, and carried folded for some time — the creases are
soft and the ink has offset slightly along the upper fold.

The printed form gives the marina's name and address at Grand Marais,
Minnesota. Completed in blue ballpoint in a hand that is legible in every
field except the last.

- **Vessel length:** 52 ft.
- **Rig:** ketch
- **Nights:** 11
- **Rate:** itemised, including an overlength surcharge
- **Paid:** in full, cash
- **Master of vessel:** illegible
- **Date:** 1981

## Examination

The receipt has been under examination since its deposit and the Archive
has not been able to find anything wrong with it.

The stock, the printing, and the form layout are correct for the marina
and the period. The marina was in business under that name, at that
address, in that season. The rate matches the schedule the marina filed
with the county, and the overlength surcharge is applied correctly, which
a forger would be unlikely to know and would have no reason to include.
The duplicate book was recovered separately in 2016 from an unrelated
source and contains the matching stub, completed in the same hand.

The signature has been examined against Vandermeer's, of which the
Archive holds nine specimens. It is illegible. It is not not his.

## Significance

The Archive dates the return of the Expedition to 1980. This receipt is
dated 1981 and describes a vessel of the Expedition's length and rig at a
port the Expedition is otherwise documented to have visited.

It is the single object standing against an otherwise consistent
documentary record, and it is the reason the return date remains an open
question. See *The Question of the Return Date*.

The Archive has been asked whether it would prefer that the receipt had
not been deposited. It would not. It has been asked this in writing, and
the reply is on file, and it is longer than it needed to be.
