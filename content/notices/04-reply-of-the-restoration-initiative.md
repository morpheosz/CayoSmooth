To the Cayo Smooth Expedition Archive

We have received the Archive's Notice of Prior Discovery and its
subsequent Response to Counterparty Assurance. We accept both, including
the correction, which was deserved.

The assurance that the material remained 100% intact was given by a
representative of ours acting in good faith and without instruction. It
was wrong. Nothing about this Expedition is intact and it was not our
place to say otherwise. We adopt the Archive's preferred formulation
without reservation and have adopted it in our own materials:

> Substantially intact, allowing for evaporation.

**On the question of discovery.** We make no claim. The Archive found the
crate. The Archive opened the crate. The Archive listened to side B and
one of its number woke believing he owned a boat, and we recognise this
as the standard of provenance in the field. The Expedition, the ketch,
the cheese manifest, the blender, and Terry are the Archive's. We have
never suggested otherwise and we will not.

**On what we are for.** We are a conservation body. Magnetic tape held
for forty years in a warehouse without climate control does not improve.
Our work is to transfer what survives into formats less susceptible to
humidity, to document the transfer, and to publish the results in a form
the public can actually hear. We are not here to rewrite the Expedition.
We are here to gently remove the mildew.

**On Terry.** We have no intention of identifying Terry. We wish to state
this in advance and in writing, because we are aware that conservation
work generates paperwork and paperwork occasionally generates answers.
Should material come into our possession that appears to identify Terry,
our position is that it will be misfiled. We consider this the
responsible course and we are prepared to defend it.

**On the blender.** We note with concern that the blender is the most
thoroughly documented object associated with the Expedition. We do not
know what to make of this. We raise it because we assume the Archive has
also noticed and has also declined to raise it.

**On licensing.** The Archive's terms are accepted in full. One (1)
frozen drink, redeemable at a marina of the Archive's choosing, at the
Archive's convenience. We would ask only that the Archive give some
notice, as our people are distributed and one of them is in Minnesota.

**On attribution.** All published material will carry the following, in
this order, without abbreviation:

> Published by the Cayo Smooth Restoration Initiative
> *By arrangement with the Cayo Smooth Expedition Archive.*

We look forward to a long association. We understand that the Archive's
counsel remains on a boat. We are content to wait, and we have taken the
liberty of assuming that the wind will change.

Cayo Smooth Restoration Initiative
